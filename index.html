The Game

The UHoop

NBBL play centers on a smaller 16-inch rim—the UHoop—a tighter target than a regulation 18-inch hoop. Without a backboard, every shot demands precision; the UHoop rewards skill and touch you can’t fake.

360° play. The rim is open from all sides. Attack from the baseline, the wings, or directly behind the hoop.

Live ball

The NBBL keeps the ball live after made baskets—it stays in play out of the net with no stoppages, and there are no timeouts. Games are built from short, high-intensity segments where every possession matters.

3-on-3. 4-on-4. Flexible formats designed for pace, spacing, and decision-making. Played on a small court (70' x 50'), compressing the game to accelerate skill development, conditioning, and intensity.

Development & NIL

The NBBL positions itself as a player development and NIL education platform, aiming to help American players distinguish their skills from those developed only in traditional basketball systems.

Family journey. Real ownership. Every rep, every run, every piece of content becomes part of a shared asset—turning time, training, and exposure into long-term value for players and the families behind them.

The Tech

The PlayCenter Platform

Hoop → Capture → Create → Distribute → Own Your Game

Instead of moments disappearing after the run, every rep is captured, organized, and turned into shareable assets that build your identity over time.

The system combines:

  • The UHoop — turning any space into a precision training lab
  • A recording app built for real basketball moments
  • A content hub to organize runs, highlights, and development
  • A growing network of PlayCenters across communities

This isn’t just play.
This is the system turning training into content—and content into long-term value.

The Gym

The NBBL Showcase Gym

NBBL has secured its first physical operating location in Gilbert, Arizona. The 7,000+ square foot facility at Eduprize Charter School becomes the first physical node in NBBL's distributed basketball infrastructure network. The gym is now accepting teams and clubs to schedule trainings at nobackboardbasketballgym.com.

The gym includes:

  • A full high school basketball court
  • Bleachers
  • A stage
  • A production bird’s nest
  • A conference room and classroom
  • Space for training
  • Events
  • Filming
  • Education
  • Community programming

This is more than a basketball gym.
It is NBBL's first physical operating node.

From this location, NBBL will deliver player development, No Backboard Basketball demonstrations, events, creator production, NIL education and content, recreational programming, and community experiences.

Gilbert is the beginning of a distributed physical infrastructure strategy designed to bring the NBBL experience into communities while connecting physical basketball activity to digital content, data and monetization.

The facility will support youth basketball training, player development, club basketball events, recreational leagues, NBBL demonstrations, high school NIL content, creator content production, basketball media production, community programming, and training and development experiences.

Gilbert is the first node in NBBL's distributed basketball infrastructure network—connecting physical basketball infrastructure, player development, content capture, digital distribution, basketball intelligence, and athlete monetization into one ecosystem.
