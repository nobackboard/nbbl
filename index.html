The Game

The UHoop

NBBL play centers on a smaller 16-inch rim—the UHoop—a tighter target than a regulation 18-inch hoop. Without a backboard, every shot demands precision; the UHoop rewards skill and touch you can’t fake.

100% skill. Without the glass, every shot must be perfect. We reward precision over power.

Live ball

The NBBL keeps the ball live after made baskets—it stays in play out of the net with no stoppages, and there are no timeouts. Games are built from short, high-intensity segments where every possession matters.

4-on-4. More space, more touches, and high-octane conditioning. No standing around.

Development & NIL

The NBBL positions itself as a player development and NIL education platform, aiming to help American players distinguish their skills from those developed only in traditional basketball systems.

360° play. The rim is open from all sides. Attack from the baseline, the wings, or directly behind the hoop.
